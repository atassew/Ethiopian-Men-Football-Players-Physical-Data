# Ethiopian-Men-Football-Players-Physical-Data
The data is collected using PlayerTek PT003 tracking system, from 66 Ethiopian Men football outfield players across 20 live matches. 
Below you will find the definitions of the metrics used. 

**Total distance covered:** Represents the volume of exercise (walking, running) measured in Kilometers 

**High-intensity running distance (sprint distance):** The sum of the distance covered when in speed zones 4 and 5

**Player load:** 	The sum of the accelerations across all axes of the internal tri-axial accelerometer during movement. It takes into account the instantaneous rate of change of acceleration and divides it by a scaling factor (divided by 100). 

**Power plays:** It is a significant action (such as acceleration or a high-speed running event) in which your power output is above 20 watts per kilogram for more than one second.
